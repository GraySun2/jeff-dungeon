# Stop rules: Dungeon

When a chat working with this scaffold stops and messages the PM instead of carrying on. It says which rule stopped it and gives numbered options with its pick marked.

## Stop and ask

- **A key, token or password.** The job would need one: stop and tell the PM. Never ask for it, print it or commit it.
- **A rule owned by another package.** Joining and dropping mid-run, revives, loot sharing, what a dead player does (multiplayer-adaptation); which button does what (control-mapping); what the inventory, map or status screens show (game-ui); how a monster, trap or item looks (art-design); the engine or generation tech (tech-choice). Write it as a requirement; don't decide it.
- **Two owners for one rule.** The primary and a secondary scaffold would define the same rule differently (for example, Extraction's loss model and this scaffold's death model). Ask the developer which owns it.
- **Switching the primary.** The developer wants Dungeon to replace the primary, or the reverse. That's its own decision: tell the PM.
- **Locking without the developer's words.** Camera, pacing, solo or co-op, a death model, what carries over, a tone, a floor count or a run length hasn't been accepted in the developer's own words: leave it PROPOSED. Never write LOCKED without a quote.
- **Copying a reference game.** A name, class list, item list, setting, map or numbers would come straight from Barony or another game. Ask before writing it; propose an original version.
- **Passing a gate on documents.** Anything asks to mark S2, G0 or a later gate passed because files exist. Gates pass on evidence; the PM records them.
- **Content the developer hasn't agreed to.** Something on the brief's "never shown" list, or something it doesn't cover that could be distressing: ask before writing it.
- **Cost cap.** Cost looks like going past the cap in the brief: tell the PM first.

## Ask the developer, then carry on

- **A required GDD answer is open** (questions 2, 3, 4, 9): ask one at a time, pick marked. Still unknown: OPEN.
- **Solo or co-op isn't clear.** Ask before writing death, revive and loot rules; co-op brings in Multiplayer Adaptation.
- **A death on the first floor has no tell or no counter:** say so, offer two fixes with your pick marked, and carry on with the answer.
- **Either dominance plan can't be beaten** on the first floor: say so, offer two counter-pressures, pick marked.
- **The four runs all end the same way:** say so, offer two changes, pick marked.

## Warn once, then carry on

Nothing is locked by stage (D-014). One plain warning, a line in the final report, then the work goes on:

- Used before the S1 gate: "No GDD yet: the scaffold will pick your genre for you. Answer the GDD first."
- A free pick not on the accepted list: treat it as a secondary and say so.
- No First Playtest package in the job: write the weakest assumption and the prototype question in the brief, and tell the PM the prototype brief is still needed for the S2 gate.
- The work would reach outside `docs/design/` (game code, controls, art, the scaffold folder itself): don't do it in this job; tell the PM it's a separate job.
